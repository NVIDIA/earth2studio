What this is / is not

Forecast layer: Earth2 six-hour accumulated precipitation (mm) from FCN + PrecipitationAFNO, GFS ICs. Display is bilinearly resampled from ~0.25° cells — not street-scale truth.

Terrain screening: relative low/flat index from USGS ~10 m DEM. No rainfall, soils, or drainage.

311 points: geocoded flood-related service requests (complaints), not surveyed flood depths or extents.

Road disruption: heuristic overlap of roads with high storm-total precip and high terrain screening. Not traffic modeling or inundation depth.

Not included: water depth, calibrated sewer hydraulics, emergency alerts, or verified skill scores.

Prototype for Hurricane Ida remnants (2021-09-01). OpenStreetMap basemap. Layers are exploratory research products.
